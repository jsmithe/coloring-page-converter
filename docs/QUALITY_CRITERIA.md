# Quality criteria

One default conversion behavior is in scope. Evaluate every output for:

1. Structural fidelity: preserve object placement, proportions, poses, and relationships.
2. Detail preservation: retain eyes, pupils, noses, expressions, accessories, and lettering.
3. Useful boundaries: create colorable enclosed regions without inventing detail.
4. Texture suppression: avoid paper grain, fabric, foliage noise, and compression artifacts.
5. Clean output: pure black lines and pure white interiors/background, without gray or color.
6. Print suitability: preserve aspect ratio and adequate resolution.

For each benchmark, compare original and output side by side, record lost or changed objects, inspect facial features/text, check background speckling and enclosure quality, and record parameters. Do not commit benchmark images without clear redistribution rights.
